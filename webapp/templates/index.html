Overcooked Collaboration Study

Thank you for participating in our study on human-AI collaboration!

You will play several rounds of an Overcooked-style cooking game with different AI partners. Your task is to work together to prepare dishes as efficiently as possible.

Before you begin:

  • The study takes approximately 15-20 minutes
  • You'll play 8 rounds total (2 rounds with each of 4 AI partners)
  • Use arrow keys to move and Space to interact
  • Your responses will be anonymized
