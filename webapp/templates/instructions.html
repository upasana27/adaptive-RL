Game Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully.

Game Environment

Overview

You will play a collaborative cooking game with an AI partner. Together, you must prepare and deliver salads in a shared kitchen.

For each recipe:

  • Each agent picks up a raw ingredient available to them.
  • The ingredient must be chopped.
  • Chopped ingredients are placed on a plate.
  • The completed plate must be picked up and delivered to finish the recipe

Recipes

There are three possible recipes you can prepare:

  • Recipe 1: Onion + Lettuce
  • Recipe 2: Potato + Broccoli
  • Recipe 3: Tomato + Carrot

Movement and Interaction

Movement:

  • Use Arrow Keys (↑ ↓ ← →) to move

Interaction:

  • Press Spacebar to interact with objects
  • You must be facing an object to interact with it
  • This includes picking up ingredients, chopping, and placing items

Rounds and Episodes

The task is organized into multiple rounds:

  • Each round consists of 5 episodes
  • Each episode lasts 40 seconds
  • In each episode, your goal is to successfully deliver the salad within 40 seconds

Scoring:

  • Your score is based on completed deliveries
  • The round number, episode number, and timer are displayed at the top of the game
