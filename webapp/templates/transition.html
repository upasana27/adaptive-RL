Trial Phase

You have now completed the practice round and are familiar with the controls and basic gameplay.

In this next phase, you will play 2 trial rounds to get comfortable with the game. Each trial round has 5 episodes, with each episode lasting 40 seconds.

What to Expect

  • Your AI partner may prefer different recipes
  • Work together to complete and deliver salads
  • After each trial round, you'll proceed to the next one

⚠️ Important: How Episode Success Works

Once you pick up an ingredient, the episode is only considered successful if the recipe containing that ingredient is delivered by your team.

For example, if you pick up an Onion, then your team must deliver an Onion + Lettuce Salad for that episode to count as a success.

Coordinate with your AI partner — pay attention to which ingredient they pick up, and choose a matching ingredient to complete the same recipe together.

💰 Remuneration

In the main study (after these trial rounds), you will earn bonus pay based on your performance.

A round is considered successful only if your team delivers a salad in every single episode of that round (all 5 out of 5).

You receive a bonus of $0.625 per successful round (up to $2.50 for 4 rounds), on top of the base pay of $2.50.
