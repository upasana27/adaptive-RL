Game Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully.

Game Environment

Overview

You will play a collaborative cooking game with an AI partner. Together, you must prepare and deliver salads in a shared kitchen.

For each recipe:

  • Each agent picks up a raw ingredient.
  • The ingredient must be chopped.
  • Chopped ingredients are placed on a plate in the center.
  • Pick up the completed plate and deliver it to earn points.

Recipes

There are three possible recipes you can prepare:

  • Recipe 1: Onion + Lettuce
  • Recipe 2: Potato + Broccoli
  • Recipe 3: Tomato + Carrot

Movement and Interaction

Movement:

  • Use Arrow Keys (↑ ↓ ← →) to move

Interaction:

  • Press Spacebar to interact with objects
  • You must be facing an object to interact with it
  • This includes picking up ingredients, chopping, and placing items

Study Structure

Round vs Episode:

  • Round = a block of 5 episodes with an AI partner
  • Episode = a single 40-second game session

The study consists of:

  • 1 Practice round (1 episode) - get familiar with controls
  • 2 Trial rounds (5 episodes each) - practice with AI partners
  • 4 Main rounds (5 episodes each) - the actual study with questionnaire after each round

Scoring:

  • Your score is based on completed deliveries
  • The episode number and timer are displayed during gameplay
