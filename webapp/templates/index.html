Collaborative Cooking Study

Thank you for participating in our study on human-AI collaboration! In this study, you will play a cooking game with an AI partner.

What to Expect

  • Study Duration: Approximately 15 minutes total
  • Game Rounds: You will play multiple rounds of a collaborative cooking game
  • AI Partners: You will interact with different AI partners throughout the study
  • After Each Round: You'll complete a brief questionnaire about your experience
  • IRB Approval: This study has been reviewed and approved by the Institutional Review Board (IRB) with code: 55564726

Confidentiality & Data Protection

  • Privacy: All data collected during this study will be kept strictly confidential
  • Anonymity: Your personal information will remain anonymous and will only be accessible by authorized research investigators
  • Data Use: The information will only be used for research purposes and will not be shared with any external entities
  • Your Rights: You have the right to withdraw from the study at any time without penalty

Remuneration

  • Completion Code: At the end of the study (when you click "Submit"), you will be provided with a Prolific Remuneration Code
  • Getting Paid: Please retain that code for your reference and enter it in the Prolific Interface to receive your remuneration
  • Important: Make sure to copy and save the code before leaving the study page
